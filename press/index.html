Description

Mighty Tiny Railways is a railway-building and optimization game where the railway you build determines how the railway operates. Lay tracks, connect growing towns and keep steam trains running on time.

Features

  • Schedules emerge from the railway. Journey times and scheduled departures are determined by the railway you build and the trains running on it.
  • Passengers with destinations. Passengers travel according to the promised schedule, changing trains along the way to reach their destinations on time.
  • Growing towns. Towns grow when you provide reliable, on-time rail service and fulfill their cargo demands.
  • No money. There are no finances to manage. Build an efficient railway and the towns will grow.
  • Online co-op. Invite a friend to build the railway together.
  • Modding. Change graphics, sounds, rules, maps and more.

About Mighty Tiny Games

Mighty Tiny Games is a tiny indie studio building mighty PC simulation games. Based in Lithuania, the studio is currently focused on its first title, Mighty Tiny Railways.
